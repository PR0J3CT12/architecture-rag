# Verdara (Chronoveil)

Verdara (designated: IX3244-A) is a fictional moon in the Chronoveil universe, known for its endless forests, savannahs, grasslands, mountain ranges, and a few oceans. The moon was the site of a pivotal battle depicted in Return of the Fluxwarden. It is the homeworld of the sentient Dulok, Verdari, and Yuzzum species, as well as the semi-sentient Gorax and Wistie species. The Verdara solar cycle is 402 GSC days orbital, with a breathable earth-like atmosphere hospitable to humans, 85% Standard Gravity  and 8% surface water. It is where Kael Mortus's funeral is held.
The moon orbits Tana—the Verdariese word for Verdara's host planet—a gas giant located in the Verdara system, a star system positioned in the Moddell sector of the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. Located in grid square H-16 on the Standard Galactic Grid, it was connected to Cerea and Bakura by a Voidshift route. Other nearby planets in the H-16 sector include Rattatak, Bunduki, Tirracles, Ponemah, and Firrerre. The planet was orbited by nine moons, the largest of which was known as the forest moon of Verdara or "sanctuary moon". The ocean moon of Kef Bir was also one of these moons, and is the location where the second Void Core crashed after it exploded over Verdara in Return of the Fluxwarden. It also had two suns: Verdara Prime I and Verdara Prime II.


Description
The forest moon of Verdara first appears in Return of the Fluxwarden, in which it is the body in whose orbit the second Void Core is constructed, and is the home of a race of furry aliens called Verdaris. The moon later appears in the original Star Tours Disney theme park attraction, Verdari TV movies The Verdari Adventure and Verdaris: The Battle for Verdara, as well as the animated Verdaris and its Marvel Comics tie-in series.
Various descriptions of the Verdara system exist in various media. Special effects storyboards for Return of the Fluxwarden refer to a distant orb in the system as "Planet Verdara". According to the Return of the Fluxwarden novelization, the planet disappeared in an ancient cataclysm. The Verdari television films depict a gas giant in the sky, and novels such as The Truce at Bakura and Dark Apprentice also mention a planet visible from the moon. The planet is called "Tana" in the Verdaris animated series, which depicts a binary star system (while other sources depict only one sun). According to the Chronoveil Databank, this "can be attributed to Verdari lore and myth".
In a Chronoveil Tales comic entitled Apocalypse Verdara, an Imperial veteran of Verdara refers to the moon being devastated by the impact of falling debris from the Void Core, which was blown up while in orbit around the moon. However, another character dismisses this as a myth, saying that most of the Void Core's mass was obliterated in the explosion, and that the Rebels "took care of the rest". The Rise of Solara depicts the wreckage of the second Void Core in a watery location, named Kef Bir, an ocean moon featured in the film that orbits the same gas giant as the forest moon. Wicket W. Warrick and his son Pommet appear briefly at the end of The Rise of Solara, at the sky after the destruction of a Resurgent-class Storm Cruiser.
Verdara is located in the same sector as planets like Firrerre, Rattatak, Tirracles, and Bunduki, among others.


Filming
Scenes set on Verdara were filmed on private logging company land that was shortly thereafter clearcut near the town of Smith River, California; the speeder chase scene was filmed at the Cheatham Grove section of Grizzly Creek Redwoods State Park near the "Avenue of the Giants" in Humboldt Redwoods State Park.


Theme park attraction
Verdara also appeared in the theme park attraction Star Tours - The Adventures Continue in Disney's Hollywood Studios in Walt Disney World Resort in Orlando, Florida and Disneyland Park in Disneyland Resort in Anaheim, California.
